About PINOK STAKE LIMITED

Building businesses. Creating opportunities. Expanding possibilities.

Who We Are

PINOK STAKE LIMITED is a dynamic conglomerate that builds, manages, and expands a portfolio of high-impact businesses across diverse sectors. As the parent company overseeing multiple brands, we are committed to driving transformational growth across media, education, property services, e-commerce, and strategic investments.

We believe in the power of enterprise to create opportunity, empower communities, and drive sustainable economic development across Africa.

Our Vision

To be the leading catalyst for African enterprise transformation, building sustainable ecosystems where innovation, integrity, and inclusive growth create lasting value for all stakeholders.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class solutions across media, education, property services, commerce, and investment that empower individuals, businesses, and communities to achieve their potential.

Core Values

Trust

Building relationships founded on transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct.

Innovation

Continuously evolving solutions that anticipate market needs and drive competitive advantage.

Sustainability

Creating positive impact on society and the environment through responsible business practices.

Our Business Model

PINOK STAKE LIMITED operates as a strategic holding company with a diversified portfolio of brands, each addressing specific market needs:

  • Media & News - Through PINOK247
  • Education - Through Aspire Nations
  • Property Services - Through HomeFix
  • E-Commerce - Through PINOK Marketplace
  • Strategic Investment - Through Future Ventures
